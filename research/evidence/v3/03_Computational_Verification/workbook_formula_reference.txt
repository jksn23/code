# SAW Relative Normalization Formula Reference

## Benefit: r_ij = x_ij / max(x_j)
## Cost: r_ij = min(x_j) / x_ij
## Preference: V_i = Σ(w_j × r_ij)
## Limit: NL_i = V_i × median_pembanding
